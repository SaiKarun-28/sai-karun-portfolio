# Sai Karun's Portfolio — Brag Plan

## 9-Question Rubric

1. **What is the app?** An interactive React portfolio site featuring a portal-style interface where clicking golden-bordered cards opens modal sections for projects, internships, resume, and contact info.

2. **Who is it for?** Sai Karun — showcasing data analytics, AI automation, and software engineering expertise to employers and collaborators.

3. **What's the hook?** A sleek workspace hero image with 6 glowing interactive portal cards that respond to clicks with smooth animations and modal reveals.

4. **What's the primary action?** Click a portal card → modal opens with curated content (projects, internships, resume, contact).

5. **What does it look/feel like?** Dark theme (#121922), warm gold accents (#d4af37), professional workspace aesthetic, smooth transitions, fully responsive.

6. **Strongest visual moment?** Hero portal interface filling the screen with interactive glowing cards, then clicking a card and watching clean content reveal in a modal overlay.

7. **What makes it different?** It's not a flat resume — it's a **portal interface** that feels like stepping into a digital workspace. Clicking is satisfying.

8. **Tone?** **Polished** — professional, elegant, serious. Clean design for serious work.

9. **Key stats?** 12 documented projects, interactive portal UI, responsive design, resume PDF viewer + summary, React 19 + Vite + TypeScript, dark-mode accessible.

---

## Creative Angle

**"A Portfolio Portal"** — The video shows a professional stepping into a digital workspace, then exploring an interactive interface that reveals his work, experience, and projects through smooth, satisfying interactions. The portal metaphor (clicking cards to enter different sections) is the product's core strength, so the video leads with it.

## Storyboard (20 seconds, 4 scenes)

### Scene 1 (0:00 – 0:05): Hook & Hero Portal
**Visuals:** Screen recording zoom-in on the landing page. Hero image fills viewport. 6 golden portal cards are visible in a 3×2 grid on the right side of the workspace. Camera slowly pans or zooms to emphasize the portal cards.

**Text on screen (top-center, settle ~1.5s):** "Sai Karun's Portfolio"
**Subtext (smaller, settle ~2s):** "An interactive portal interface"

**Timing:** Hero animation holds 2s, text enters smooth, ~3s more for emphasis.

**Transitions:** Fade in from black.

**SFX:** Subtle tech chime on text entry, soft ambient tone.

---

### Scene 2 (0:05 – 0:10): Projects Showcase
**Visuals:** Click on the "PROJECTS" card. Modal opens smoothly (fade + slide from bottom or overlay). Projects section displays 12 project cards in a responsive grid with titles, descriptions, and tech tags. Quick scroll/pan through the grid to show depth.

**Text on screen (fade in ~0.5s settle ~2.5s):** "12 Documented Projects"
**Subtext:** "Data analytics • AI automation • Software engineering"

**Timing:** Modal reveal ~1s, text settle ~2s, scroll/pan ~2.5s, hold on final frame ~0.5s.

**Transitions:** Smooth fade + scale of modal, smooth scroll through projects.

**SFX:** Subtle whoosh on modal open, soft upbeat music pulse on text.

---

### Scene 3 (0:10 – 0:15): Experience & Resume
**Visuals:** Close the modal (fade out). Click on "RESUME" card. Resume modal opens. Show the resume summary paragraph and the embedded PDF viewer below. Camera slowly scrolls down to show both sections.

**Text on screen (fade in ~0.5s settle ~2s):** "Professional Summary & Resume"
**Subtext:** "View, download, or explore directly"

**Button highlights (subtle glow/pulse):** "Open PDF in new tab" and "Download PDF" buttons are visible and emphasized.

**Timing:** Modal reveal ~1s, text settle ~2s, scroll down ~1.5s.

**Transitions:** Smooth fade of previous modal, slide/fade of resume modal, scroll.

**SFX:** Soft tech chime on modal open, subtle document-page turn sound on scroll.

---

### Scene 4 (0:15 – 0:20): Call to Action & Live Deployment
**Visuals:** Close modal. Pull back to show the full portal interface once more (hero image + all 6 cards visible). Highlight the GitHub and LinkedIn cards with a subtle glow/pulse. At the bottom of the screen, display the live URL and deployment info.

**Text on screen (fade in ~0.5s settle ~2s):** "Explore it live today"
**URL display (appear ~1.5s settle ~2s):** `https://SaiKarun-28.github.io/sai-karun-portfolio/`
**Badge/badge (appear ~2.5s):** "Built with React 19 + Vite + TypeScript"

**Timing:** Pull-back animation ~1s, text settle ~1.5s, URL appear ~1.5s, hold final frame ~1.5s.

**Transitions:** Smooth zoom-out, fade in of text/URL, subtle pulse on GitHub/LinkedIn cards.

**SFX:** Soft tech sound on URL reveal, upbeat music swell for finale.
