# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Sai Karun's Interactive Portfolio

## Objective
Create a polished 20-second launch-style brag video showcasing Sai Karun's interactive portfolio site, emphasizing the portal interface design, projects, resume, and responsive interactivity.

## Output
- Composition directory: `composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag.mp4`
- Format: Landscape 16:9 (1920×1080)
- Duration: 20 seconds
- Poster frame: Hero portal interface with glowing cards (~2–3s mark)

## Source Material
- Project root: `D:\Portfolio`
- Primary files read: `index.html`, `src/styles/global.css`, `package.json`, `src/components/PortalShell.tsx`, `src/data/projects.ts`, `public/assets/hero.png`
- Product name: **Sai Karun's Interactive Portfolio**
- Tagline / strongest claim: **"A portfolio portal—click to explore"**
- Key UI moment: Hero image filling viewport, 6 golden portal cards (GitHub, LinkedIn, Projects, Internships, Resume, Contact) visible and interactive. Clicking a card reveals content in a smooth modal overlay.
- Copy that must appear verbatim:
  - "Sai Karun's Portfolio"
  - "An interactive portal interface"
  - "12 Documented Projects"
  - "Data analytics • AI automation • Software engineering"
  - "Professional Summary & Resume"
  - "View, download, or explore directly"
  - "Explore it live today"
  - `https://SaiKarun-28.github.io/sai-karun-portfolio/`
  - "Built with React 19 + Vite + TypeScript"

## Creative Direction
- **Tone preset:** Polished
- **Creative direction:** Professional, elegant, serious. A workspace portal that feels interactive and satisfying.
- **Interpretation:** Polished tone means measured pacing, clean typography, no loud transitions, emphasis on clarity and professional design. Subtle animations, smooth reveals, no chaotic energy.
- **Angle:** A professional stepping into a digital workspace, then exploring an interactive interface that reveals his work, experience, and projects through smooth, satisfying interactions. The portal metaphor (clicking cards to enter different sections) is the product's core strength.
- **Hook (0–2s):** Hero image with portal cards fill the screen. "Sai Karun's Portfolio" text enters smooth and settles.
- **Outro/punchline (18–20s):** Pull back to show full portal interface. GitHub & LinkedIn cards pulse subtly. Live URL appears with "Built with React 19 + Vite + TypeScript" badge. Music swell.
- **Avoid:**
  - Generic SaaS language ("streamline," "optimize," "unlock")
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Unrelated redesigns

## Visual Identity
- **Background:** Dark (`#121922`)
- **Text (light):** `#f5f5dc` (off-white/cream)
- **Accent:** Gold (`#d4af37`)
- **Secondary:** Semi-transparent panels with gold borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`)
- **Display font:** Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui (clean, modern, sans-serif)
- **Body font:** Same as display
- **Visual references from project:**
  - Hero image (`public/assets/hero.png`): young professional at desk in warm-lit workspace with shelves/plants
  - 6 portal cards arranged in 3×2 grid: GitHub (top-left), LinkedIn (top-right), Projects (middle-left), Internships (middle-right), Resume (bottom-left), Contact (bottom-right)
  - Modal overlays with semi-transparent dark backgrounds
  - Project grid (12 project cards with titles, descriptions, tech tags)
  - Resume section (summary text + PDF viewer embed)
  - Smooth transitions, hover effects (subtle gold glow)

## Storyboard

**Scene 1 (0:00–0:05): Hook & Hero Portal**
- Hero image fills viewport with 6 golden portal cards visible
- Text "Sai Karun's Portfolio" enters and settles (large, centered, gold accent)
- Subtext "An interactive portal interface" fades in below
- Camera slowly emphasizes portal cards (subtle zoom or pan)
- SFX: Tech chime on text entry, soft ambient tone

**Scene 2 (0:05–0:10): Projects Showcase**
- Click on "PROJECTS" card, modal opens (smooth fade + scale)
- Projects grid displays 12 project cards; quick scroll/pan to show variety
- Text "12 Documented Projects" enters and settles
- Subtext "Data analytics • AI automation • Software engineering" fades in
- SFX: Whoosh on modal open, soft upbeat music pulse

**Scene 3 (0:10–0:15): Experience & Resume**
- Close projects modal, click "RESUME" card
- Resume modal opens, shows summary paragraph + embedded PDF viewer
- Camera scrolls down slowly to show both sections
- Text "Professional Summary & Resume" enters and settles
- Subtext "View, download, or explore directly" fades in; button highlights visible
- SFX: Tech chime on modal open, subtle document turn on scroll

**Scene 4 (0:15–0:20): Call to Action & Deployment**
- Close modal, pull back to show full portal interface once more
- GitHub & LinkedIn cards pulse with subtle glow
- Text "Explore it live today" enters and settles
- Live URL appears: `https://SaiKarun-28.github.io/sai-karun-portfolio/`
- Badge "Built with React 19 + Vite + TypeScript" appears below URL
- SFX: Tech sound on URL reveal, upbeat music swell for finale

## Composition Notes

- Keep typography clean and readable; no fast-flashing text
- Ensure all text settles for at least 0.8–2s depending on length
- Show actual UI from the portfolio (hero image, portal cards, project grid, resume modal)
- Use the project's actual copy and color palette
- Transitions should be smooth (fades, slides, scales) — no jarring cuts
- Final poster frame: hero portal with glowing cards (best moment for thumbnail)
